# Professional Monochrome Redesign

## Goal
Rebuild the full Dhanraj interface in a refined black-and-white visual system that feels credible, smooth, and effortless for industrial buyers to navigate.

## What will change
- Replace the teal/sage theme with a high-contrast monochrome design system across every page, control, data view, and 3D scene.
- Redesign the header and opening section around a clearer value proposition, stronger trust signals, and direct quote/call actions.
- Simplify section layouts, cards, tabs, tables, forms, and navigation so product details, rates, coverage, and contact options are easier to scan.
- Rework the product, network, rates, testimonials, FAQ, contact, and Pet Coke page presentation into one cohesive professional system.
- Refine spacing, typography, borders, photography treatment, and restrained motion for a premium but practical industrial feel.
- Preserve all existing business copy, datasets, SEO markup, working calculators, downloads, search, WhatsApp, call, map, and 3D interactions.

## Usability and quality
- Keep important actions visible and clearly labeled on desktop and mobile.
- Maintain accessible contrast, keyboard focus, reduced-motion behavior, stable layouts, and readable data tables.
- Verify the complete experience in desktop and mobile viewports, including navigation, product selection, rate filtering, quote preselection, and the contact form.

## Technical details
- Define all monochrome colors as semantic tokens in the shared stylesheet and synchronize procedural Three.js colors through the shared palette module.
- Keep content sourced from typed files in `src/data` and preserve explicit sitemap decisions and existing route metadata.
- Update only presentation and interaction code; no business rules or content values will change.
